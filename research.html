Research

My research interests are in development, public, and political economics. I am particularly interested in questions related to poverty, agency, bureaucracy, and corruption.

01

Development economics

Household welfare, poverty, vulnerability, and the constraints that shape economic opportunities.

02

Public economics

Public service delivery, state capacity, policy implementation, and the incentives facing public institutions.

03

Political economy

Agency, bureaucracy, corruption, and the relationship between institutions and individual behavior.

Research papers and project details will be added as they are ready for public release.
